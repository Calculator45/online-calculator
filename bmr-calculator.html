What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate is an estimate of how much energy your body needs to perform basic functions such as breathing, circulation and maintaining body temperature while at rest.

Method used Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

BMR does not include calories used through walking, exercise, work or other daily activity.

BMR is an estimate and individual energy requirements can vary.
