How loan payments are calculated

A standard loan payment depends on the amount borrowed, the interest rate and the length of the loan. Payments are usually made monthly.

Monthly payment formula M = P × [r(1 + r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]

P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.
