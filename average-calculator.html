How to calculate an average

The arithmetic average, also called the mean, is calculated by adding all values together and dividing the total by the number of values.

Average formula Average = Sum of Values ÷ Number of Values

Example

For the numbers 10, 20 and 30, the total is 60. There are three numbers, so:

60 ÷ 3 = 20

What does the average tell you?

The average, also called the arithmetic mean, represents a central value for a group of numbers. It is calculated by adding all the numbers together and dividing the total by the number of values.

Averages are commonly used to compare exam scores, prices, measurements, expenses, temperatures and many other sets of numerical data.

Average, minimum and maximum

The average shows the central value of the numbers, while the minimum is the smallest value and the maximum is the largest. Looking at these values together can give you a better understanding of the overall range of the data.
