How calorie needs are estimated

This calculator first estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate, which is the amount of energy your body uses at rest. It then applies an activity factor to estimate your total daily energy expenditure.

Calculation Daily calories = BMR × Activity Factor

About the result

The maintenance value estimates the calories needed to maintain your current weight. The weight-loss and weight-gain figures shown here use a simple adjustment of 500 calories per day.

These values are estimates only. Individual calorie needs can vary due to health, body composition, medications and other factors.
