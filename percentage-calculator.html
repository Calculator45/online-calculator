How percentages work

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

Formula Percentage × Number ÷ 100

Example

To calculate 20% of 500:

20 × 500 ÷ 100 = 100

How do percentages work?

A percentage represents a part of a whole using 100 as the base. For example, 20% means 20 out of every 100. To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the number.

Common uses of percentages

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, interest rates, taxes, exam scores, price changes and many other everyday calculations.

For example, if an item costs $80 and is discounted by 25%, the discount is $20, so the final price would be $60.
