How the savings goal calculator works

The calculator subtracts the amount you have already saved from your target and divides the remaining amount by the number of months available.

Monthly savings (Goal − Current Savings) ÷ Number of Months

Example

Suppose your goal is 50,000 and you have already saved 5,000. If you want to reach the goal in three years, you have 36 months to save the remaining 45,000.

45,000 ÷ 36 = 1,250 per month

How to use your savings goal result

The monthly savings amount shows how much you need to set aside each month to reach your target within the selected time period. If the required amount is too high for your budget, you can extend the time available, reduce the target amount, or increase your starting savings.

Breaking a large savings goal into monthly or weekly amounts can make the target easier to plan for and track over time.
