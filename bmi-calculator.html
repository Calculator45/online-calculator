What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that compares a person's weight with their height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure for weight categories in adults.

Formula BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m)

Adult BMI ranges

Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 and above Obesity

BMI is a general screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or diagnose a health condition.
