How ideal weight is estimated

This calculator uses the Devine formula, which estimates ideal body weight from height and sex.

For men 50 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet
For women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet

Ideal weight formulas provide general estimates only. Healthy body weight can vary depending on body composition, age, muscle mass and other individual factors.
