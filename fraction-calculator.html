How fraction calculations work

Fractions can be added, subtracted, multiplied or divided. The result is automatically simplified to its lowest terms.

Example 1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4

When adding or subtracting fractions, the calculator first finds a common denominator. For multiplication and division, the numerators and denominators are calculated directly.

How fraction calculations work

A fraction represents part of a whole. The top number is called the numerator and the bottom number is called the denominator. For example, in 3/4, 3 is the numerator and 4 is the denominator.

When multiplying fractions, multiply the numerators together and then multiply the denominators together. When dividing fractions, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

Why simplify fractions?

Simplifying a fraction expresses the same value using the smallest possible numerator and denominator. For example, 6/8 can be simplified to 3/4.
