About Online Calculator

Online Calculator was created to make everyday calculations quick, simple and easy to understand.

Our website provides free online calculators for finance, health, mathematics and everyday tasks. Whether you need to calculate a loan payment, percentage, BMI, age, interest or the difference between two dates, our goal is to give you a straightforward tool without unnecessary complexity.

Our Goal

We aim to provide useful calculators that are fast, accessible and easy to use on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Alongside each calculator, we aim to explain what is being calculated and how the calculation works so that the result is easier to understand.

Our Calculators

The calculators available on this website cover several categories, including money and finance, health, mathematics, dates and other common everyday calculations.

We continue to improve the website and plan to add more useful tools over time.

Important Information

The calculators and information provided on Online Calculator are intended for general informational and educational purposes. Results may be estimates and should not replace professional financial, medical, legal or other specialist advice where such advice is required.
