What is compound interest?

Compound interest means that interest is calculated on both the original amount and the interest that has already been added. Over longer periods, this can cause money to grow faster than with simple interest.

Compound interest formula A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ

A is the future value, P is the starting amount, r is the annual interest rate, n is the number of times interest compounds each year, and t is the number of years.
